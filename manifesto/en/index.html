A Search Engine That Doesn't Search for You

by Carlo Esposito
Eyed Softwares / Aploide Softwares

We live in a world where searching means being searched. Every word typed, every thought entered into a search bar, every uncertainty turned into a query is tracked, stored, profiled. The promise of simplicity and speed has come at a steep price: our cognitive freedom.

Eyed Search was born to break this pattern.

Not as a nostalgic reaction or a niche product, but as a new architecture: an independent digital infrastructure, built from scratch in Europe to provide a space where searching no longer means being surveilled.

Beyond the Engine: The Dignity of Search

Google has won. Not only through efficiency, but through deep integration into our lives. It has become the lens through which we view reality. And like all lenses built by a few, it distorts.

But online search isn't just a technical service. It is a human act, vulnerable, private. It's where we seek cures, answers, doubts, identities. It's where much of our relationship with knowledge is shaped. An infrastructure that governs all this cannot be owned by a single private entity.

Eyed Search isn't an alternative. It's a re-foundation.

Speed Without Compromise

Eyed Search runs inside Eyed Out, our private browser. It's lightweight, native, fast. Users open it an average of 21 times a day, without push notifications, rewards, or artificial stimuli.

Our goal isn't addiction, but positive habit: opening a window to the world that returns not ads disguised as results, but essential, clear, and immediate information.

With smart bangs, category search, and Otus, our integrated AI assistant, every search becomes more powerful without becoming more monitored.

Privacy as a Foundation, Not a Feature

Most so-called “privacy-first” search engines actually rely on Google or Bing. They are masks, not engines. Economically and technically dependent on the very systems they claim to oppose.

Eyed Search is different. It is a sovereign engine. It has its own stack, its own algorithms, its own crawling network. It does not rely on third-party APIs. It doesn't use personal data. It doesn't create profiles. It doesn't watch users.

In Eyed Search, ads are based only on the real-time query, not on who you are or what you've done. GDPR is not a checkbox. It's the ground we build on.

A Public Infrastructure in Private Hands?

Online search is now critical infrastructure: used by journalists, students, activists, doctors, and ordinary citizens. But whoever controls search controls access to truth, or at least to what appears to be true.

Europe is responding with regulations like the Digital Markets Act and GDPR, but what's missing is an ethical engine: a project that proves tech can be built without surveillance.

Eyed Search aims to be that engine.

The Market Exists. The Urgency Too.

The global online search market is worth over $280 billion. Google holds nearly 90%. DuckDuckGo, relying entirely on Bing, has 0.7% of the market.

If a truly independent engine like Eyed Search captured even 0.1%, the potential revenue would be around $133 million annually.
But beyond numbers, what matters is the message: users are ready to switch, if someone truly offers an alternative.

We're not at idea stage. Our MVP is live. Otus is operational. The browser scales. Every day, hundreds of users join an ecosystem that grows, evolves, and doesn't monitor them.
