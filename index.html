Some of what we've built

A few of our products, one shared rule: they answer to you, and to no one else.

  • Spettro A terminal-first coding assistant built on agents you can watch work — planning, coding, reviewing — with nothing hidden. spettro.app
  • Spettro Data Open, live analytics on which AI models are winning real usage inside Spettro, week by week. data.spettro.app
  • Sussurro Push-to-talk dictation that never leaves your machine. Speak once, get clean text in any app. sussurro.eyed.to
  • Eyed Out A lightweight private browser. Fast, native, and blind to what you do with it. aploi.de/eyed-out
  • Eyed Pass A private vault for the things that must stay yours. aploi.de/eyed-pass

House rules

  • Open where we can. Most of our code is public — trust you can verify. Where it isn't, the other two rules still hold.
  • Local-first. If it can run on your machine, it does. Your voice, your keys, your browsing stay home.
  • No surveillance. No trackers, no analytics, no ad profiles. This page loads nothing from anyone else.

Eyed is the new name of Aploide — same hands, same principles, no longer a software house. We're moving from aploi.de to eyed.to slowly, without breaking your links.
