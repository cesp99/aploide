Privacy Policy

Introduction

This document explains how our applications handle data and outlines our commitment to protecting user privacy. Our applications are designed to collect the minimum data needed to operate, and we provide this policy to ensure transparency and trust.

The data controller is: Carlo Esposito, operating through Eyed® Softwares ("Eyed®", "Eyed™", "we", "us", "our"). You can reach our privacy contact at privacy@aploi.de.

Spettro by Eyed™

Spettro is a terminal-first multi-agent coding assistant. It is composed of several pieces with different privacy characteristics: the Spettro CLI that runs on your machine, the Spettro Backend that we operate as an LLM inference service, the web application at spettro.app, and the desktop apps Spettro-Desktop (Linux and Windows) and Spettro for macOS.

What we collect and why

When you sign in to Spettro's cloud features, we process the following personal data:

  1. Email address — obtained from our authentication provider at the moment you complete sign-in. We use it as the account identifier and to render account screens.
  2. Subscription state — your current plan, billing status, and billing period. This is updated via our payment provider.
  3. API key (hash only) — every ep_… key we issue is stored exclusively as a hash; the plaintext is shown to you once and never persisted by us.
  4. Per-inference usage row — for each successful LLM call, we record the model alias, token counts, the cost computed from our pricing table, the API key id used, and the timestamp. We use this exclusively to enforce your monthly credit budget and to display your usage.

We do not store your chat history, your prompts, or the LLM's responses on our servers.

LLM request forwarding

Chat messages and tool results sent through Spettro are forwarded, verbatim, to upstream model providers to generate a response. The current set of providers is:

  • Spettro (SuperFast, SuperSmart) — our models, governed by this policy.
  • Alibaba Cloud — default for most models; governed by Alibaba Cloud's privacy policy.
  • MiniMax — governed by MiniMax's privacy policy.
  • Crof AI — governed by Crof AI's privacy policy.

These providers may log, retain, or further process your messages in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

Local storage on your device

The Spettro CLI keeps conversation state, agent memory, and other artifacts strictly on your machine. Conversations, API keys (stored in encrypted form), configuration, memory files, custom commands and skills, and checkpoint history remain on your device. Nothing about the conversation leaves your computer except for inference calls to upstream providers.

Spettro-Desktop (Linux & Windows)

The desktop app is a thin view over the CLI subprocess. It does not duplicate secrets. It stores user preferences, chat-session snapshots, and — if you enable the LAN phone relay — records of paired devices locally. The desktop renderer does not embed analytics or third-party trackers.

Spettro for macOS

Spettro for macOS is a desktop application built entirely for macOS using Swift and SwiftUI. It functions the same as Spettro-Desktop — it is a view over the CLI subprocess — but, unlike Spettro-Desktop, it is not based on Electron: it is a native app that leverages macOS-native frameworks. It stores the same types of data as Spettro-Desktop (preferences, chat-session snapshots, paired-device records) in macOS-native locations. It does not duplicate secrets and does not embed analytics or third-party trackers.

Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account, including your email, subscription linkage, API keys, and usage records, by using the dashboard https://spettro.app/dashboard or writing to privacy@aploi.de. We action deletion requests within 30 days. Local artifacts on your machine (encrypted keys, conversation history, memory, checkpoints) are unaffected and remain under your control.
