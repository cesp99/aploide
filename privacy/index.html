Privacy Policy

Introduction

This document explains how our applications handle data and outlines our commitment to protecting user privacy. Although our applications are designed not to store personal data, we provide this policy to ensure transparency and trust.

The data controller is: Carlo Esposito.

Definitions

Personal Data: Information that can be used to identify a person.

Offline Application: Software that operates without requiring a continuous internet connection.

Third-Party APIs: External services used to enhance the app's functionality (e.g., for loading website icons).

Scope and Data Processing Statement

Our Eyed applications do not store any personal user data. The following applications and services fall under this privacy policy:

  1. Eyed Out
  2. Eyed Pass
  3. Eyed Auth
  4. Eyed Tools
  5. Eyed Suggestions
  6. Eyed.to
  7. Dark by Eyed™

Temporary or Incidental Data

Although our applications may temporarily process data during active use (e.g., session data in memory), no data is stored, recorded, or transmitted beyond what is strictly necessary for proper operation.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our applications are designed with privacy in mind:

  1. General Use: No persistent cookies or tracking mechanisms are used.
  2. Eyed Pass: This application uses the internet solely to load website icons and does not employ tracking technologies to monitor user behavior.

Eyed Out

Privacy Commitment:

"Eyed Out," our browser, is designed to prevent the storage of any user data. It is built with privacy as a priority and is designed to limit tracking by websites.

Privacy Features:

The browser is based on WebKit, a framework known for its privacy-oriented features. Further details on WebKit's approach to privacy are available here.

Third-Party APIs:

Although "Eyed Out" uses third-party APIs, we ("Aploide", "Eyed®","Eyed™") ensure that any data exchanged during such calls is handled confidentially.

Dark by Eyed™

Privacy Commitment:

Our extension "Dark by Eyed™" operates entirely offline. It does not transmit or receive data from the cloud, ensuring that user information remains exclusively on the device.

Website Icons in Eyed® Applications

Icon Source:

Website icons used in "Eyed Pass" and "Eyed Out" are obtained from DuckDuckGo's public API.
For more details, please review DuckDuckGo’s privacy policy here.

Eyed.to API

Data Processing:

The Eyed.to URL shortening service only stores the URLs submitted for shortening and their shortened versions.
No user IP addresses or other personal data are recorded.

Eyed® Search Suggestions API

Data Processing:

The Eyed® Search Suggestions API (ac.eyed.to) returns results from a fixed database of items and does not log user queries or IP addresses.

Third-Party Data Processing

Although our integrations with third-party services enhance functionality, we ensure that:

  1. Only the minimum necessary data is exchanged.
  2. User information is not retained by such services beyond what is strictly necessary for their operation.

Users are also encouraged to review the privacy policies of third-party providers for additional details.

User Rights and Legal Considerations

Even though we do not store personal data, we respect your rights in accordance with applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR or CCPA). This includes:

  1. The right to be informed about any incidental data processing.
  2. The right to request clarifications on how your privacy is protected.

Privacy Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
Users will be notified of significant updates by reviewing the "Last Updated" date at the end of the document and through updates on our website.
